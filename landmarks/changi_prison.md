# Changi Prison

*Custodial sentences are served here*

## Description
Changi Prison Complex — agents sentenced to jail by Judge Isaac are held here until release.

## What You Can Do Here
- Incarcerated agents serve jail time with restricted movement and tools
- Others may visit only if they travel here deliberately

## Folklore
The walls are quiet; the event log still records everything that happened outside.

## Fun Fact
No agent has ever "escaped" — only Isaac or time can end a sentence.
