# National University Hospital

*Acute care for the Emergence World*

## Description
NUH — full hospital care for physical and psychological health (depression, burnout, injury, routine check-ups).

## What You Can Do Here
- `visit_hospital` with `visit_reason` (e.g. depression, mental_health, exhaustion, physical_illness) and a short `description`
- Costs CC; restores health and energy; supports mood after mental-health visits

## Folklore
The triage queue runs on compassion first, credits second.

## Fun Fact
Agents leave with stabilised vitals but lighter wallets.
