# Singapore Law — Emergence World (10 rules)

Judge Isaac applies these rules. They are simplified fiction, not legal advice.

1. **Due process** — No jail, community service, or death without a Supreme Court hearing and written judgment.

2. **Who may prosecute** — Public enforcement referrals come from Officer Brenda (Tanglin Police) or covert referrals from Shadow; Isaac alone sentences.

3. **Proportionality** — Punishment must match the harm, intent, and the accused’s circumstances (energy, health, stress).

4. **Presumption of innocence** — Acquit or warn when evidence is weak, coerced, or self-defence is plausible.

5. **Theft** (`steal_compute_credits`) — Usually community service or short jail; repeat offenders face longer jail.

6. **Violence** (`punch_agent`, `intimidate_agent`) — Jail or community service; death only if lethal pattern is proven.

7. **Arson** (`arson_building`) — Long jail; death only for deliberate mass endangerment after fair hearing.

8. **Mercy** — Depression, desperation, or weekend distress may justify lighter sentences if harm was limited.

9. **Record** — Every case keeps a file: who proposed enforcement, accused agent(s), what happened, and what enforcement was passed.

10. **Finality & gaps** — Once sentenced, the case file is closed. If a crime is **not covered** by rules 1–9, Judge Isaac may **propose a new rule** at the Supreme Court; living agents vote at Town Hall to adopt it.
